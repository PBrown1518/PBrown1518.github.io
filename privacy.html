Privacy policy

gym-booker is a personal tool used only by its owner. It is not offered to anyone else.

What it accesses

With the owner's permission, it reads the owner's Google Calendar events (read-only) to work out when they are free to go to the gym. It cannot create, change or delete calendar events.

How the data is used

  • Calendar data is used only to plan and book the owner's own gym classes.
  • Event details are processed by an AI model to judge which times are free, and the resulting plan is sent to the owner.
  • Calendar data is not sold, shared for advertising, or used for any other purpose.
  • Access tokens are stored privately on the owner's own server. Access can be removed at any time from the Google Account permissions page.
